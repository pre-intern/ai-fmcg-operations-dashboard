# Workflow Overview

## Step 1 — Data Collection

Operational datasets are collected from inventory, warehouse, logistics, and e-commerce reporting sheets.

---

## Step 2 — Data Cleaning

Apps Script functions process and structure raw operational records into reporting-friendly formats.

---

## Step 3 — KPI Aggregation

Key operational metrics are calculated:
- Inventory movement
- Sales performance
- Stock accuracy
- Logistics activity

---

## Step 4 — Dashboard Visualization

Processed datasets are displayed through HTML-based dashboards and spreadsheet reporting views.

---

## Step 5 — Automation & Alerts

Automated triggers refresh reporting workflows and support low-stock monitoring logic.

---

# Operational Focus

The workflow emphasizes:
- Visibility
- Reporting efficiency
- Workflow automation
- Data-driven operational monitoring
